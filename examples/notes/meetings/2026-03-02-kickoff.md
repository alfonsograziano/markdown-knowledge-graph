# Apollo kickoff

[[maria]] showed the embedding prototype. It answers in about 150 ms on the staging cluster, inside the 200 ms budget.

[[tom]] proposed to measure success on the "no results" rate, not on clicks, because clicks reward bad titles.

## Decisions

- The A/B test starts on 16 March and runs for two weeks.
- The team will use hybrid search: keyword and vector scores combined. See [vector search notes](../notes/vector-search.md).
